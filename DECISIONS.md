# MenQ Webpage — Decisions / Որոշումներ

## Հայերեն
- **W-D001:** Webpage-ը governed consumer է Locked D-025-ի նկատմամբ։
- **W-D002:** Hero, gradient, grid, glass և marketing composition-ը Product Extension են։
- **W-D003:** Design migration-ը չի փոխում API/DB/auth/business semantics-ը։
- **W-D004:** Maturity promotion-ը evidence-based է։
- **W-D005:** Root + `docs/menq-standard/` package-ը canonical authority-ն է։
- **W-D006:** Runtime contract changes-ը պահանջում են separate transaction։
- **W-D007:** Canonical docs-ը HY/EN equal meaning են։

## English
- **W-D001:** Webpage is a governed consumer of Locked D-025.
- **W-D002:** Hero, gradient, grid, glass, and marketing composition are Product Extensions.
- **W-D003:** The design migration does not change API, database, authentication, or business semantics.
- **W-D004:** Maturity promotion is evidence-based.
- **W-D005:** Root plus `docs/menq-standard/` is the canonical authority package.
- **W-D006:** Runtime-contract changes require a separate transaction.
- **W-D007:** Canonical documentation carries equal HY/EN meaning.
