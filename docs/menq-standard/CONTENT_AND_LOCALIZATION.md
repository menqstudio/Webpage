# Content and Localization / Content և localization

## Հայերեն
HY/EN/RU product locales-ը route-level supported են։ Canonical governance-ը HY/EN է։ Copy keys-ը typed dictionary contract ունեն։ Missing translation-ը silent fallback չպետք է դառնա production truth։ SEO metadata, legal content և conversion copy-ն locale-aware են։

## English
HY/EN/RU product locales are supported at route level. Canonical governance is HY/EN. Copy keys follow a typed dictionary contract. Missing translations must not silently become production truth. SEO metadata, legal content, and conversion copy are locale-aware.
