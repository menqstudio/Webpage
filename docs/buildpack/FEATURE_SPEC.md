# Feature Spec

## 1. Header
### Requirements
- Sticky header.
- Text logo placeholder.
- Navigation links.
- Language switcher for HY / EN / RU.
- Theme switcher for dark/light mode.
- Primary CTA button.

### Navigation items
- Գլխավոր
- Լուծումներ
- Ոլորտներ
- Ինչպես ենք աշխատում

- On scroll, header gains elevated background using header tokens.

- CTA scrolls to form.

## 2. Hero
### Requirements
- Strong business headline.
- Clear subheadline.
- Primary and secondary CTAs.
- Trust line.
- Visual: dashboard + AI workflow network.

### Primary message
The company creates AI, automation, CRM, web, dashboard, and internal system solutions that reduce costs, save time, attract customers, and improve control.

## 3. Pain / business loss section
### Requirements
Show the hidden cost of unstructured processes.

Pain cards:
- manual repetitive work,
- scattered data,
- lost sales opportunities,
- weak visibility,
- unnecessary costs,
- dependence on people instead of systems.

## 4. Solution overview
### Requirements
Explain that the company does not simply “make websites” or “write software”; it builds business systems.

Core pillars:
- Software solutions,
- AI automation,
- Web and online presence,
- Business process automation,
- System integration,
- Analytics and management.

## 5. Service blocks
### Requirements
Services must be grouped into four business blocks.

Block 1: Solutions that bring new customers.
Block 2: Solutions that simplify internal management.
Block 3: AI solutions for business.
Block 4: Integration and growth infrastructure.

Each block must show:
- title,
- business goal,
- included services,
- business value.

## 6. Business success key
### Requirements
This is the strategic positioning section.

Main idea:
Business growth depends on controlled, measurable, automated systems.

Key points:
- each action has an owner,
- each stage has a status,
- each delay is visible,
- repetitive steps can be automated,
- decisions can be based on data,
- management can see real state in one screen.

## 7. Industries and functions
### Requirements
Industries must be grouped, not listed as one long wall.

Groups:
1. Sales and customer-driven businesses.
2. Service and field operations businesses.
3. Education and healthcare.
4. Finance, accounting, and professional services.
5. Manufacturing, warehouse, logistics, and supply.

## 8. Results
### Requirements
Show business outcomes.

Cards:
- cost reduction,
- time savings,
- new customer acquisition,
- controlled work,
- fewer errors,
- scalable system.

## 9. AI as business tool
### Requirements
Explain AI pragmatically, not as hype.

Capabilities:
- data analysis,
- document processing,
- customer request classification,
- smart recommendations,
- workflow decision support,
- automated reporting,
- anomaly detection,
- internal AI assistants.

## 10. How we work
### Requirements
Step-by-step process:
1. Business analysis.
2. Solution mapping.
3. Design.
4. Build.
5. Implementation.
6. Measurement and improvement.

## 11. Why choose us / trust
### Requirements
No fake proof. No forced certificate gallery yet.

Trust points:
- officially certified specialists,
- AI-accelerated solutions,
- deep business process analysis,
- custom approach, not template work,
- long-term support and development,
- privacy and security awareness.

## 12. FAQ
### Requirements
Include conversion-supporting questions.

Recommended FAQ:
1. What kind of businesses can use these solutions?
2. Do we need to replace our current systems?
3. Can you work with existing CRM/tools?
4. How long does implementation take?
5. Is AI safe for business data?
6. Can we start with a small solution?
7. Do you provide support after launch?
