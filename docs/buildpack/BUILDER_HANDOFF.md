# Builder Handoff

## Mission
Build a premium public landing page for a company that provides AI automation, CRM, ERP/internal management systems, websites, e-commerce, dashboards, workflow automation, integrations, AI assistants/chatbots, document automation, mobile apps, branding/UI, support, and maintenance.

## Most important instruction
Do not stop because of missing real-world values. Use placeholders and log unresolved items in `OPEN_ITEMS_LOG.md`.

## Non-negotiables
1. Design system must be fully tokenized.
2. No hardcoded visual values in components.
3. Build must support dark/light mode.
4. Build must support HY / EN / RU locales.
5. Armenian copy must stay Armenian; English copy English; Russian copy Russian.
6. Testimonials and case studies are not included now.
7. Certificate display should be cautious and not require public proof yet.
8. Lead form must send to Email + Telegram.

## Recommended stack
- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- CSS variable tokens
- Component-based architecture

## Page sections to build
1. Header
2. Hero
3. Pain / business loss
4. Solution overview
5. Services grouped into 4 business blocks
6. Business success key
7. Industries and functions
8. Business results
9. AI as business tool
10. How we work
11. Why choose us / trust
12. FAQ
13. Final CTA + lead form
14. Footer

## Design direction
Premium AI business tech.

Dark mode:
- dark navy,
- electric blue,
- cyan,
- glass cards,
- glowing lines.

Light mode:
- white clean,
- blue accent,
- dark contrast sections.

Visual style:
- tech-driven,
- AI-driven,
- slightly futuristic,
- not too much,
- not playful,
- not template-looking.

## Hero visual
Use dashboard + AI workflow network combination.

Must show abstract business control:
- metrics,
- workflow nodes,
- AI insight,
- automation status.

No real client data.
