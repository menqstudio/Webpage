# MVP Scope

## MVP goal
Launch a premium public landing page that explains the company’s AI, automation, and software solutions and converts visitors into consultation leads.

## MVP must achieve
The first release must make the visitor understand:
1. what the company does,
2. what business result the client receives,
3. what solutions are offered,
4. which industries are served,
5. why the company is trustworthy,
6. how to request a consultation.

## Included in MVP
### Pages
- One public landing page.
- Optional thank-you state after form submission.
- Privacy/security note section inside the page or footer.

### Languages
- Armenian as primary release language.
- English and Russian ready in structure and routing.
- Initial English/Russian content can be placeholder-translated later, but the architecture must support localization from day one.

### Sections
1. Header
2. Hero
3. Business loss / pain section
4. Solution overview
5. Services grouped into 4 business blocks
6. Business success key
7. Industries and functions
8. Business outcomes / results
9. AI as a business tool
10. How we work
11. Why choose us / trust
12. FAQ
13. Final CTA + lead form
14. Footer

### Core services to present
The service list must be grouped into four business-oriented blocks rather than shown as a flat technical list.

#### 1. Solutions that bring new customers
- Web websites
- Landing pages
- Lead forms
- CRM
- Campaign support
- Sales automation
- E-commerce / online shop
- Sales and lead management systems

#### 2. Solutions that simplify internal management
- ERP / internal management systems
- Dashboards
- Workflow automation
- Task management
- Inventory / warehouse management
- HR and team management systems
- Booking and scheduling systems
- Customer support / ticketing systems
- Custom admin panels

#### 3. AI solutions for business
- AI automation
- AI assistants
- Chatbots
- Document automation
- AI reporting
- AI support
- Customer request classification
- Data extraction
- Smart recommendations

#### 4. Integration and growth infrastructure
- System integrations
- APIs
- Payment integrations
- Accounting integrations
- Notification / SMS / Email automation
- Cloud deployment
- Support and maintenance
- Analytics and KPI dashboards

## Excluded from MVP
- Real testimonials.
- Real case studies.
- Public certificate gallery with real certificate images.
- Complex client portal.
- Blog.
- Pricing calculator.

## MVP conversion goal
Primary conversion is a submitted consultation form or contact action through Telegram/WhatsApp/phone/email.

## MVP success indicators
- Visitor understands offer within 10 seconds.
- CTA is visible above the fold.
- Services are understandable without technical background.
- Form can be submitted successfully.
- Lead notification arrives to Email and Telegram.
- Page works well on mobile.
- Page has no hardcoded visual values.
